I. Aurora SendCloud WhatsApp Business Configuration Tutorial
Welcome to the powerful WhatsApp marketing features of Aurora SendCloud. This guide will walk you through every step, from account integration to sending marketing campaigns. Please read carefully and follow the instructions.
Part One: Integration and Account Binding
This section will guide you on how to connect your WhatsApp Business Account (WABA) with the Aurora SendCloud platform.
Step 1: Navigate to the [Integration] Module
First, please log in to your Aurora SendCloud platform account.
In the left-side navigation bar of the main platform interface (Overview page), find and click on the fourth icon, [Integration].

<Image border={false} src="https://files.readme.io/d331f19ddcb726dc4ed0fec27c33bcfb28af87e3cf01c67ed6c4563aa2327080-image.png" />

<br />

Step 2: Begin the WhatsApp Connection
On the "Integration" page, you will see the WhatsApp option.
Click the blue [Connect] button to the right of WhatsApp to start the integration process.

<Image border={false} src="https://files.readme.io/2f94ea6e445d2079d793dc4b99ed76a289a83a8a8528d936d3d2e06429d55e3c-image.png" />

<br />

Step 3: Authorize through Facebook Login
After clicking "Connect," the page will automatically redirect to the WhatsApp Business Account registration/connection guide provided by EngageLab.
Click the [Login with Facebook] button on the page.

<Image border={false} src="https://files.readme.io/fc0f37173d11d4a781ce4791cb6d0b48b82642d56ca487b0e763811e2329698b-image.png" />

<br />

A Facebook login window will pop up.

<Image border={false} src="https://files.readme.io/f3a99ebe6e929d8c2901da5d3916254082da8dd64df3aba8a08cbc77397081ec-image.png" />

<br />

Important Note: Please ensure you log in with the Facebook account that you use to manage your Meta Business Manager. Your WhatsApp Business assets must be associated with this Facebook account to successfully complete the subsequent binding process.
Step 4: Complete the Authorization
After successfully logging into your Facebook account, an authorization prompt window will appear, indicating that you are about to begin setting up your WhatsApp Business Account. Please click the [Get Started] button in the bottom-right corner.

<Image border={false} src="https://files.readme.io/054254492697583ac9c7d5dbc90a4bfaf77a77f56614001f34bbcf4624f9bd19-image.png" />

<br />

Next, the system will display a list of permissions that you will be granting to EngageLab. Please click the [Continue] button in the bottom-right corner to complete the authorization. This is a required step.

<Image border={false} src="https://files.readme.io/7ab3d3e38fa93a9958fadd41af6ae4d9c232b77319bf2251d6f20d6ab6e3fd57-image.png" />

<br />

Step 5: Create or Select a Meta Business Account
On this page, you can either select an existing Meta Business Account or create a new one.
If you have not created one before, please select "Create a Business Account" and fill in the following information as required:
a. Company Name
b. Company Email
c. Company Website or Business Page
d. Country/Region

<Image border={false} src="https://files.readme.io/17137ad02598c0415c506b478d4337034b27b39179d9fcb8e9c315863603d819-image.png" />

<br />

Important Considerations:
e. Company Email: It is strongly recommended to use your company's corporate email address (e.g., [yourname@yourcompany.com](mailto:yourname@yourcompany.com)). Using personal email addresses like QQ or 163 is discouraged, as this helps to improve the account approval rate.
f. Company Website: Please provide the official website address of your company that is accessible and showcases your legitimate business operations. Meta will review this address. If your website's server is located in China, please ensure that your firewall is configured to allow access from international IP addresses. Otherwise, Meta's review robots will be unable to access the site, leading to review failure.
Step 6: Create or Select a WhatsApp Business Account (WABA)
Similar to the previous step, you can either select an existing WABA or create a new one.

<Image border={false} src="https://files.readme.io/6a7528085dd786df99a09499b122c7fb32588fa4e0a836d606fc2b44e26a181b-image.png" />

<br />

If you are creating a new account, you will need to fill in the following information:
a. WhatsApp Business Account Name: This is the name used for your own internal management.
b. WhatsApp Business Profile Display Name: This is the important business name that your customers will see in WhatsApp.
c. Time Zone: Select the time zone for your business's location. Please note that the time zone cannot be changed once it has been selected.
d. Category: Select the industry your business belongs to.
e. Business Description: Briefly describe your company.

<Image border={false} src="https://files.readme.io/58cf438c876157b3d6b91a6d34426b823aca2bddc3ad2b444e1da8d61a22c48e-image.png" />

<br />

Step 7: Add and Verify the Phone Number
This is the most critical step, where you need to link a dedicated phone number to your WABA.
Number Requirements:
a. It must be a brand new number that has not been registered with any personal or business version of WhatsApp.
b. It must be able to receive text messages or voice calls to get the verification code.
Procedure:
a. Select the country/region code (e.g., US +1 for the United States).
b. Enter your phone number.
c. Choose the verification method: [Text Message] or [Phone Call].

<Image border={false} src="https://files.readme.io/3ef0f0e7a2e2f738c50483e1cb1a19e4242ea665ce297172758c768b9667046b-image.png" />

<br />

Recommendations:
d. We strongly recommend selecting Voice Call as the verification method, as it has a higher success rate.
e. If you are using a phone number from Mainland China (+86), you may fail to receive the verification code due to carrier restrictions on international calls/SMS. If you do not succeed after multiple attempts, we recommend checking your phone's blocking settings or contacting your carrier to enable the relevant permissions. Alternatively, consider using a phone number from a different region.
Enter the 6-digit verification code you receive to complete the verification.

<Image border={false} src="https://files.readme.io/7e3d9c06d9532249907b9576c711582109f4b5e16ae604c8bfe3db81b70d1b28-image.png" />

<br />

Step 8: Complete the Integration
Once the verification is successful, the system will automatically complete all configurations. The page will then redirect back to the Aurora SendCloud "Integration" page. You will now see the status for WhatsApp displayed in green as [Connected], indicating that the integration was successful.

<Image border={false} src="https://files.readme.io/ed5e8cc97ed6fc12dd6b2c12808d61dc1d29dee46580e72d9ae620b7313ff550-image.png" />

<br />

Part Two: Creating WhatsApp Message Templates
All promotional messages sent via the WhatsApp API must use pre-approved templates.
Step 1: Navigate to the WhatsApp Template Management Page
In the left-side navigation bar, click [Content].
In the expanded sub-menu, select [WhatsApp].
Step 2: Create a New Template
On the WhatsApp Templates page, click the [+ New Template] button in the upper-left corner.

<Image border={false} src="https://files.readme.io/4d4552f4b7a8bb3091e2a113388fb9c96ae9150333ed1fe942eda380dbc335d0-image.png" />

<br />

Step 3: Complete the Basic Template Information
Template Category: Defaults to Marketing.
Template Name: Name the template. Only numbers, lowercase letters, and underscores are supported.
Language: Select the language for the template. You can click Add more languages to create multilingual versions of the same template.

<Image border={false} src="https://files.readme.io/a1c5a9472471748c135b1d57aefeb35ef5480c7d9b5728ec338d3137b1617a84-image.png" />

<br />

Step 4: Edit the Template Content
Header: Optional.
Body: Required. This is the core content of your message.
Footer: Optional. Typically used for a short signature or note.
Buttons: Optional, but highly recommended to increase the interaction rate. There are two types:
a. Quick Reply: When a user clicks this button, a preset text message is sent directly to you.
b. Call to Action: Can be set to either "Call Phone Number" or "Visit Website."
For the "Visit Website" action, for example, you need to configure the button's display text (e.g., "Shop Now") and the destination URL that the user will be directed to upon clicking.

<Image border={false} src="https://files.readme.io/4a7e8c821c9943ff69d625155593835b6332d47ec16729867c66d78a22c9d8a4-image.png" />

<br />

Step 5: Submit for Review
After you have finished editing all the content, click the [Create] button in the top-right corner.
Your template will be submitted to Meta for official review. This process typically takes anywhere from a few hours to several business days.
Once approved, the status of the template will change to [APPROVED]. Only templates with this status can be used for sending.

<Image border={false} src="https://files.readme.io/423237f7100b949951dcdbfc6d9d1bde86171b8d5035eb160d00050a7a23dc1e-image.png" />

<br />

Part Three: Test Sending
Once the template is approved, it is recommended to send a test to ensure that everything is functioning correctly.
In the template list, find a template that has been approved and click [Test] in the actions column on the right.

<Image border={false} src="https://files.readme.io/0c17dc2d8810e5cb30c67489e0ce64cc8ef5d8a5facfd7e5759220de0d6c1e6b-image.png" />

<br />

In the pop-up test window, enter the recipient's mobile number.
Format requirements: The number must be in the complete +[Country/Region Code]+[Mobile Number] format (e.g., +852xxxxxxxx). Formats starting with "00" or those without a country code are not supported.

<Image border={false} src="https://files.readme.io/e163b794bcbbe005b616a73442548ab1792b3fa3dbb0f434779b26a10865733c-image.png" />

<br />

Click [Send]. If the configuration is correct, the recipient's WhatsApp will receive this test message.

<Image border={false} src="https://files.readme.io/4cfb86e3440df2357722a5599e1b1cd55d7df17c784a8e296cbb369daaecdd3a-image.png" />

<br />

Part Four: Create and Send a WhatsApp Marketing Campaign
After successful testing, you can create a formal marketing campaign to send bulk messages to your contacts.
Step 1: Create a New Marketing Campaign
In the left-side navigation bar, click [Marketing] to go to the [Campaigns] page.
Click the [Create] button.

<Image border={false} src="https://files.readme.io/630be8945a6ff6c0cda1691b60ad34180e377f1ba1ebca6b9478d17835755866-image.png" />

<br />

Step 2: Select WhatsApp Task
In the "Create Campaign" pop-up window, first enter a Task Name for this campaign.
Then, select the Task Type as WhatsApp Task.
Click [Save].

<Image border={false} src="https://files.readme.io/de16a67cf00cb6c283fe1c503516922a1f26147e82e827e05d5e98bae53a185d-image.png" />

<br />

Step 3: Configure Campaign Details
Recipients: Click “Add Recipients” and select the contact group or tags you want to send to. Please ensure this contact data includes a correctly formatted mobile number field.
Sender: The system will automatically select your connected WhatsApp sender number.
WhatsApp Template: Click “Select Template” and choose an approved (APPROVED) template and its corresponding language version from the list.
Execution Time: You can choose to “Send Immediately” or set a future “Scheduled Send” time.
Once all settings are configured, click the [Save] button at the bottom of the page to complete the creation of the marketing campaign.

<Image border={false} src="https://files.readme.io/d7a7da2916edc1a2536055a68ccdc5a4142f12aa634fbcbb217de6b0a77bcc06-image.png" />

<br />

A critical reminder
WhatsApp's requirements for permission-based (Opt-in) marketing and message compliance are extremely strict. You must ensure that all contacts you message have explicitly consented to receive your communications. Never send spam or harassing messages, as this may result in your number being banned. We wish you great success and effective campaigns! If you have any questions, please do not hesitate to contact our customer support team.
