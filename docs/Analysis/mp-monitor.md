# What is MP Monitor

<Image align="center" alt="MP Monitor" border={false} caption="MP Monitor" src="https://files.readme.io/948724cd1ce55938b3c30b5a32b4c7bed148989df824373dd8209ad2db3f6bae-image.png" />

MP Monitor (Mailbox Provider Monitor) is a dedicated monitoring feature for feedback data from Mailbox Providers. Within this feature section, you can view information related to Feedback Loops and other complaint reporting. After completing the configuration, you can also obtain reputation scores generated by mailbox providers for your domain and IP address, as well as the number of spam complaints counted in MP feedback.

**Key Benefits:**
* Provides direct support for email delivery optimization, serving as a practical tool to improve email sending performance
* Delivers valuable data analysis results, offering insights for optimizing email performance (such as delivery and inbox placement rates)
* Helps improve email delivery and inbox placement rates while reducing the likelihood of emails being marked as spam or blocked

## Google Postmaster

Google Postmaster provides valuable analytics insights that can optimize your email performance, improve your email deliverability, and increase the chances of your messages reaching your audience's inbox. You can access information such as Spam Rate, IP Reputation, Domain Reputation, Feedback Loop, Authentication, Encryption, and Delivery Errors for the emails you send.

### Google Postmaster Setup

After completing this guide, it may take a few hours to link your Postmaster Tools account and start collecting data.

1. Register and log in to the <Anchor label="Google Postmaster Tool" target="_blank" href="https://postmaster.google.com/">Google Postmaster Tool</Anchor>
2. In the Google Postmaster Tool, add and verify the domain you want to configure:
   1. Click the "+" button in the bottom right corner of the homepage, enter your domain name, and click "Next"
   2. Add the generated TXT record value to your domain's DNS server configuration, then click "Verify"
3. Add SendCloud's monitoring email "sendcloudfbl@gmail.com":
   1. Hover over your verified domain on the homepage, click the "More⋮" button on the right, then select "Manage Users"
   2. Click the "+" button in the bottom right corner of the page and add the Aurora SendCloud monitoring email address: sendcloudfbl@gmail.com

<Callout>
  Aurora SendCloud has read-only access and will not have any adverse effects on your business.
</Callout>

## Yahoo CFL

Yahoo Complaint Feedback Loop helps you identify opportunities to improve how well your emails are received and protect your sending reputation. This allows you to optimize your target audience and sending frequency. You can monitor and aggregate user spam complaints from Yahoo to protect your sender reputation and improve inbox placement rates.

### Yahoo CFL Setup

1. Select one of your sending domains that already exists in Aurora SendCloud
2. Copy the generated TXT record value and add it to your domain's DNS server configuration
3. Click the "Verify" button

  You may need to wait a few minutes before Yahoo recognizes your changes to the TXT records.
