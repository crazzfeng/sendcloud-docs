# Email Reading Analytics

Unlock powerful insights into your audience engagement with comprehensive email reading analytics. Monitor critical metrics like reading time, click rates, and geographical distribution to optimize your email campaigns and drive better results.

<br />

## Powerful Filtering Options

Dive deep into your email performance data with comprehensive filtering capabilities:

<Accordion title="Available Filter Dimensions" icon="filter">

| **Filter Type**    | **What It Does**                                                               | **Best Used For**                    |
| :----------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------- |
| **Email Type**     | Separate Trigger emails (transactional) from Batch emails (marketing campaigns) | Comparing transactional vs marketing performance |
| **API_USER**       | View performance metrics for specific API users                                | Multi-tenant applications or team analysis |
| **Label**          | Filter by custom labels you've assigned to email sends                         | Campaign categorization and tracking |
| **Campaign**       | Analyze specific email campaign performance                                    | Campaign optimization and comparison |
| **Request Period** | Select your analysis timeframe (default: last 7 days)                         | Trend analysis and historical comparison |

</Accordion>

### ⏰ Optimal Timing Analytics

Maximize engagement with data-driven send time optimization:

- **Identify peak engagement hours** for your specific audience
- **Optimize send times** for maximum open and click rates  
- **Segment timing strategies** by audience demographics
- **Improve campaign ROI** through strategic scheduling
