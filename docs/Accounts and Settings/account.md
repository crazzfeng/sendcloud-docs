# Account Management

Manage your account settings, security preferences, and personal information from one centralized location.

## Getting Started

Access your account settings by clicking your profile picture in the bottom-left corner and selecting **"Info"** from the dropdown menu.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Account Security">
    
<Accordion title="Email Address" icon="envelope">
Your email address is your primary login credential and where you'll receive important notifications.

**To update your email:**
1. Click **Modify** next to your email field
2. Verify your current email with the 6-digit code sent to your inbox
3. Enter your new email address
4. Verify your new email with the code sent to that address
5. Click **Save**

> ⚠️ Your new email must be valid and not already associated with another account.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Password" icon="key">
Keep your account secure by regularly updating your password.

<Columns layout="auto">
  <Column>
    **I know my current password**
    1. Click **Modify** next to Password
    2. Enter current password
    3. Create new password
    4. Confirm new password
    5. Click **Confirm Change**
  </Column>
  <Column>
    **I forgot my password**
    1. Click **Forgot password?** on login page
    2. Enter your registered email
    3. Use the verification code sent to your email
    4. Set your new password
    5. Click **Confirm Reset**
  </Column>
</Columns>

**Password Requirements:**
- 6-16 characters long
- No spaces allowed
- Must include at least 3 of these: lowercase letters, uppercase letters, numbers, special characters (!@#$%&*)
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Phone Number" icon="mobile">
Add your phone number for enhanced security and SMS notifications.

**First-time setup:**
1. Click **Connect** next to Phone
2. Select your country code
3. Enter your phone number
4. Click **SMS Verification** and enter the code received
5. Click **Confirm**

**To change your number:**
1. Click **Modify** next to Phone
2. Verify your current number with SMS code
3. Enter new number with country code
4. Verify new number with SMS code
5. Click **Confirm Change**

> 📱 Each phone number can only be linked to one account.
</Accordion>

  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Regional Settings">

<Accordion title="Understanding Regions" icon="map">
Regions are geographically distributed data centers that help optimize performance for your users worldwide. Each region operates independently with its own resources, data, and billing.

**Key Points:**
- Resources and quotas are separate per region
- Data doesn't automatically sync between regions  
- Services must be purchased separately for each region
- Billing is calculated independently per region
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Available Regions" icon="server">
**Default Region:** Singapore
Automatically activated when you create your account.

**Additional Regions:**
- **US (Silicon Valley)** - Optimized for Americas
- **CN (Hong Kong SAR)** - Optimized for Greater China

**To activate a new region:**
1. Go to Info → Regions
2. Select region from "Add Region" dropdown
3. Click **Add** and confirm
4. Begin using services in the new region

> 💡 Region activation is free, but you'll be charged for services used within each region.
</Accordion>
