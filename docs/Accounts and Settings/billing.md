# Billing

Aurora SendCloud provides transparent, usage-based billing for your email delivery needs. This page explains how our billing system works, what you're charged for, and how to manage your payments.

## How Billing Works

We charge for email delivery based on actual delivery results, not just emails sent. This ensures you only pay for successful email processing and delivery attempts.

### Billing Cycle

- **Monthly billing**: Charges are calculated monthly based on your usage
- **Consumption tracking**: Real-time monitoring of your email delivery volume
- **Detailed reporting**: Monthly consumption reports with itemized details

## Billing Result Types

Your charges are based on the following delivery result categories:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Delivered">
    Successfully delivered emails to the recipient's inbox. These are emails that reached their intended destination without any issues.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Soft Bounce">
    Temporary delivery failures that may be retried. Examples include:
    - Mailbox temporarily full
    - Server temporarily unavailable
    - Message size too large
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Invalid Email">
    Permanent delivery failures including:
    - **Format Error**: Malformed email addresses
    - **Not Exist**: Email addresses that don't exist
    - **Reject**: Emails rejected by the recipient server
    - **Junk Mail**: Emails marked as spam or junk
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Understanding Your Bill

Each monthly bill includes:

### Itemized Breakdown
- **Delivered emails**: Count and cost per delivered email
- **Processing fees**: Charges for soft bounces and invalid email processing
- **Volume tiers**: Different rates based on your monthly sending volume
- **Additional services**: Any premium features or dedicated IP costs

### Payment Information
- **Due date**: Payment terms and when payment is due
- **Payment methods**: Accepted payment options (credit card, bank transfer, etc.)
- **Late fees**: Information about any applicable late payment charges

## Pricing Structure

<Accordion title="Volume-Based Pricing" icon="calculator">
Our pricing scales with your usage:

- **0-10,000 emails**: Standard rate per email
- **10,000-100,000 emails**: Reduced rate for higher volume

Contact our sales team for enterprise pricing and volume discounts.

</Accordion>

## Important Notes

> **📋 Billing Transparency**
> 
> We believe in transparent billing. All charges are clearly itemized, and you can track your usage in real-time through your dashboard.

> **⚠️ Payment Terms**
> 
> Payments are due within 30 days of invoice date. Late payments may result in service suspension and additional fees.

> **💡 Cost Optimization**
> 
> To optimize your email costs, maintain good list hygiene and monitor your delivery rates. Higher delivery rates result in lower overall costs.
