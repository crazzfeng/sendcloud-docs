## 🌐 Set Up Your Sending Domain

Your sending domain is your email identity - it's what recipients see in the "From" field and what email providers use to verify your authenticity.

### Why This Matters

<Accordion title="Domain Benefits & Impact" icon="lightbulb">
  
**Deliverability**: Verified domains significantly improve your email delivery rates and reduce spam filtering.

**Trust & Branding**: A custom domain builds recipient confidence and reinforces your brand identity.

**Reputation Management**: Establish and maintain a positive sender reputation with email providers.

</Accordion>

### Quick Setup Steps

1. **Add your domain** in the Domains section
2. **Add DNS records** provided by Aurora SendCloud to your domain registrar
3. **Verify ownership** - we'll automatically check your DNS settings
4. **Start sending** once verification is complete (usually takes 5-15 minutes)

> 💡 **Pro Tip**: Use a subdomain like `mail.yourcompany.com` to keep your main domain reputation separate from email sending.

## 🔑 Understand Your API_USER

Your API_USER is your secure authentication key for accessing Aurora SendCloud's services.

### What It Does

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Authentication">
    Securely connects your applications to Aurora SendCloud's email infrastructure
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Tracking">
    Links email statistics and analytics to your specific account and usage
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Rate Limiting">
    Manages your sending quotas and ensures compliance with your plan limits
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

### Security Best Practices

- 🔒 **Never share** your API_USER in public repositories or forums
- 🔄 **Rotate regularly** - update your credentials every 90 days
- 📱 **Use environment variables** to store credentials in your applications
- 👥 **Create separate API_USERs** for different applications or team members
